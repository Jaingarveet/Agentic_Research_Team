# Run Results & Execution Trace

This directory contains artifacts and screenshots from the execution of the Agentic Research Team pipeline for the global Small Modular Reactor (SMR) industry analysis.

### User Input: 
`Target audience: Energy policy analysts and investors seeking a practical overview rather than deep engineering details.` 

`Research the current state of the global small modular reactor (SMR) industry. Compare the leading SMR developers across North America, Europe, and Asia, covering reactor designs, deployment timelines, costs, licensing progress, and safety features. Assess government support, private investment, and the main barriers to commercial adoption. Include a comparison table and finish with a balanced evaluation of whether SMRs are likely to become commercially competitive over the next 20 years.` 

`NOTE: Find the actual contents generated by the interview sub-graph and content-compilation node in the content.text and the actual pdf of final output after latex compilation in test_4.pdf` 

## Key technical highlights for this run: 

* **End-to-End Pipeline Visualization & Token Metrics:** Demonstrates the full LangGraph lifecycle across 3 execution turns, consuming ~500.2K tokens for a total run cost of ~$0.1041 over ~1,088 seconds (~18.1 minutes) .

* **Parallel Sub-Graph Interview Loop:** Illustrates the map-reduce architecture spawning 4 dynamic `conduct_interviews` sub-graphs concurrently across SMR regulatory, market, technical, and regional domains . Each analyst executes an iterative workflow: query generation $\rightarrow$ web search $\rightarrow$ expert response grounding .

* **Self-Healing LaTeX Error Recovery & State Checkpoints:** Highlights state persistence and active self-healing during final document generation . The pipeline detected compilation failures and autonomously ran 4 correction cycles (`latex_error_attempts: 4`) between `latex_compiler` and `latex_validator` before committing to Overleaf .

## Key Highlights from the output:

* **Multi-Regional Regulatory Comparison:** Evaluates nuclear licensing regimes across North America, Europe, and Asia, focusing on Part 53 risk-informed frameworks, NRC design certifications, and EU multi-country joint reviews .

* **Deployment Levers & Policy Reforms:** Identifies core deployment accelerators, including standard design maturity taxonomies, harmonized pre-licensing pathways, serial multi-unit licensing, and cross-border vendor qualification .

* **10-Year Utility Roadmap & Financial Trajectory:** Provides a phase-gated rollout framework (0–12m Foundation, 12–36m Pilot Execution, 36–60m Scaling) paired with 0–5 year and 5–10 year risk-adjusted LCOE projections .

* **Compilation & Reference Self-Healing:** Demonstrates system behavior during complex document compilation, resolving layout syntax while managing unparsed citation keys (e.g., `[?]`)  and escaped special characters in references (`SessionSpID $i=40$`) .
